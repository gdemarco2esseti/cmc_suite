# Fazzini, "Analisi di bilancio" (Wolters Kluwer/IPSOA, 4a ed. 2015) - capability list for a controllo di gestione system

Note: many formulas in the source markdown are images and were lost in extraction. Where a formula is not printed, it was reconstructed from the text and checked against the book's own worked numbers (example company "Alfa", 4 years). Sezioni cited as "cap./par." of the book. Thresholds are the book's empirical Italian values and are always "to be compared to sector benchmark".
